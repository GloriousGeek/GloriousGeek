I’m passionate about technology, business and building things that solve real problems.

My journey started in Civil Engineering, expanded into Data Science and has taken me across industries including renewable energy, healthcare technology, software development and business operations.

Along the way, I’ve developed an interest not just in how technology works, but in why products exist, who they serve and what makes people actually want to use them.

I enjoy approaching problems from both sides: understanding the technology behind a solution and experiencing it through the eyes of the person using it.

💡 **How I Think**

I believe good technology should make life simpler, not more complicated.

Whether I’m exploring an idea, automating a process or developing a digital product, I try to think beyond the technical implementation.

* Consumer first: Would I actually use this? Does it solve a genuine problem? Is the experience intuitive?
* Business-minded: Can an idea become something useful, sustainable and commercially viable?
* Engineering approach: Understand the problem, question assumptions, build, test and improve.
* Always curious: Experiment with emerging technologies, challenge existing approaches and keep learning.

I’m particularly interested in the intersection of technology, entrepreneurship, artificial intelligence and user experience.
